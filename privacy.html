Redo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

English

Redo is an offline handbook for crisis preparedness in Sweden. The app does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your bookmarks, checklist ticks and language choice are saved only on your own device (browser storage) and are never sent anywhere. You can remove them by clearing the app's data.

The optional compass uses the phone's orientation sensor only on the device; no data leaves the phone. Links to external websites (for example krisinformation.se or 1177.se) open those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

The content is general information and does not replace medical advice or instructions from Swedish authorities. In an emergency call 112.
